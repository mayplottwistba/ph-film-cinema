Hello!

This site aims to archive all Cinemalaya films from 2005 to the present, along with details on where they can be watched.

This project would like to thank all the individuals who have compiled lists of films available on different streaming platforms, as well as those who have uploaded external links for films that have no known source as of the moment.

Please also understand that this site is still under development, and some information may contain errors or inconsistencies. If you have any suggestions, corrections, or questions, feel free to drop me a message on X: @mayplottwistba

Update: Listed are films that can be streamed from YouTube, Netflix, JuanFlix, CCP site, Prime Video, Apple TV, iWant, and VivaOne.

For external links, migrated films are up to those released in the year 2011. But as much as possible, please stream these movies legally if there are sources available. Mabuhay ang pelikulang Pilipino.

The developer would like to thank all contributors. No copyright infringement is intended. All film titles, posters, descriptions, names, trademarks, and other copyrighted materials belong to their respective owners. This site is intended solely as an archival and informational project.
